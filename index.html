05 / ABOUT

Why
propulsion?

I am interested in mechanical engineering because I enjoy understanding how physical systems work — particularly systems where thermodynamics, fluid mechanics and mechanical design interact.

Propulsion is where many of those disciplines come together. A turbofan is not simply a collection of components; it is a tightly coupled thermodynamic, aerodynamic and mechanical system.

I am using personal projects to build the knowledge and practical skills needed to eventually work in mechanical propulsion engineering.

The goal of this portfolio is not to present myself as an expert. It is to document the process of learning, building, testing assumptions and improving my engineering understanding over time.
